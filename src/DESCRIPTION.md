# McSquishy: Blob on the Run

**McSquishy: Blob on the Run** is a light-hearted side-scrolling platform game where the player takes control of McSquishy, a small sentient blob trying to make his way through a strange and hazardous world.

McSquishy may not look like much, but his soft and squishy body allows him to overcome obstacles that would stop an ordinary hero. The player must guide him through a series of platforming challenges, avoiding hazards, navigating the environment, and reaching the end of each level.

The game is designed around simple, accessible controls and short levels that can be played directly in a web browser. The focus is on movement, timing, and increasingly challenging platforming rather than complex systems or a detailed story.

The visual style should be colourful, playful, and slightly absurd, with McSquishy’s exaggerated blob-like movement and reactions giving the game much of its personality.

The overall goal is to create a small, easy-to-understand platform game that can be picked up quickly while still giving players a satisfying challenge as they progress.
